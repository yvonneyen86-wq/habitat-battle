> Hello from Class 701
> Hi! My name is Amy Chen. I am twelve years old. I am a new student at Zhubei Junior High. My class is 701. I am very happy today.
> This is my friend Leo. He is in my class too. Leo is tall and funny. He isn't a good singer, but he is a great basketball player.
> Ms. Lin is our English teacher. She is kind, and her class is fun. Kevin and Ben are my classmates. They are twins! Kevin is next to the window, and Ben is near the door.

# grammar
GT | Be verbs: am / is / are
R | **I am** · **you / we / they are** · **he / she / it is** → I am Amy. They are twins.
R | Negative: **is not = isn't** · **are not = aren't** · **I'm not** → Leo isn't a singer.
R | Question: **Be + subject ...?** → Is Ms. Lin your teacher? Yes, she is.
R | Short forms: **I'm · you're · he's · she's · it's · we're · they're** → She's kind.

> The Lost and Found Box
> Ms. Lin has a big box in her classroom. It is the Lost and Found Box. Today, the box is full!
> "What is this?" Ms. Lin asks. It is a blue pencil case. "That is my pencil case!" says Amy. "Thank you!"
> "And what are these?" Ms. Lin asks. These are two red rulers and an eraser. "Those are Leo's rulers," says Kevin. "But the eraser isn't Leo's."
> Next, Ms. Lin finds an umbrella, three notebooks and an orange. "Is this your umbrella, Kevin?" "Yes, it is!" Kevin says. "And these notebooks are mine too."
> Nobody wants the orange. "Hmm, this orange is very old," Ms. Lin says. The children laugh. Now the box is almost empty. Ms. Lin puts the box next to the door.

# grammar
GT | This / that / these / those + a / an
R | **this / that** + one thing → This is a pencil. That is an apple.
R | **these / those** + two or more things → These are pencils. Those are boxes.
R | **a** + consonant sound · **an** + vowel sound → a ruler, an eraser, an umbrella
R | Plurals: **-s** · **-es** · **-y → -ies** · **child → children** → books, boxes, dictionaries, children
