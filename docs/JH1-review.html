Grammar 1（國一）U1–U8 課綱補寫單元：審核用

這 8 個單元沒有老師講義，由 Claude 依國一課綱撰寫。遊戲裡學生看到的內容全是英文；本頁中文只是給老師看的標籤。

U1　Be Verbs: am / is / are

How do we say who we are?

🧩 文法公式卡：Be verbs: am / is / are

  • I am · you / we / they are · he / she / it is → I am Amy. They are twins.
  • Negative: is not = isn't · are not = aren't · I'm not → Leo isn't a singer.
  • Question: Be + subject ...? → Is Ms. Lin your teacher? Yes, she is.
  • Short forms: I'm · you're · he's · she's · it's · we're · they're → She's kind.

重組句：

  1. My name is Amy, and I am twelve.
  2. Leo is tall and very funny.
  3. Kevin and Ben are not in Class 702.
  4. Is Ms. Lin your English teacher?
  5. We are happy to be at school.
  6. My mom is a doctor in Zhubei.

🔤 單字

🧑‍🎓 student stu/dent　👩‍🏫 teacher teach/er　👫 classmate class/mate　🤝 friend fr/iend　👦 brother broth/er　👧 sister sis/ter　👩‍⚕️ doctor doc/tor　😊 happy hap/py　😴 tired t/ired　🍔 hungry hun/gry　🎂 twelve tw/elve

📖 短文：Hello from Class 701

Hi! My name is Amy Chen. I am twelve years old. I am a new student at Zhubei Junior High. My class is 701. I am very happy today.

This is my friend Leo. He is in my class too. Leo is tall and funny. He isn't a good singer, but he is a great basketball player.

Ms. Lin is our English teacher. She is kind, and her class is fun. Kevin and Ben are my classmates. They are twins! Kevin is next to the window, and Ben is near the door.

My family is at home now. My mom is a doctor, and my dad is a cook. My brother is ten. Are you a new student too? Please write to me!

  1. 選擇 Which class is Amy in? → Class 701 （Class 702／Class 801）
  2. 選擇 What is Leo good at? → basketball （singing／drawing）
  3. 選擇 Where is Kevin in the classroom? → next to the window （near the door／behind Amy）
  4. 完整句 What is Amy's dad's job? → Amy's dad is a cook.
  5. 完整句 Is Ms. Lin kind? → Yes, Ms. Lin is kind.
  6. 完整句 Are Kevin and Ben in Amy's class? → Yes, Kevin and Ben are Amy's classmates.
  7. 口說 What is your English teacher like?
  8. 口說 Who is in your family?

❓ 題卡（12 張）

  1. 📖 課文 How old is Amy Chen? → Amy Chen is twelve years old.
  2. 📖 課文 Who is Ms. Lin? → Ms. Lin is Amy's English teacher.
  3. 📖 課文 Are Kevin and Ben brothers? → Yes, Kevin and Ben are twin brothers.
  4. 📖 課文 What is Amy's mom's job? → Amy's mom is a doctor.
  5. 🧩 文法 How old are you? → I am thirteen years old. 提示：am · years old
  6. 🧩 文法 Is your English teacher kind? → Yes, my English teacher is kind. 提示：is · kind
  7. 🧩 文法 Are your classmates noisy today? → No, my classmates aren't noisy today. 提示：aren't · noisy
  8. 🧩 文法 Is Leo a good singer? → No, Leo isn't a good singer. 提示：isn't · singer
  9. 🔤 填空 Amy is a new ___ at Zhubei Junior High. → student
  10. 🔤 填空 Ms. Lin is our English ___. → teacher
  11. 🔤 填空 Kevin and Ben are my ___. → classmates
  12. 🔤 填空 I am ___, so I want a sandwich. → hungry

⚔️ Battle 題

  1. 改錯 I is a student. → I am a student.
  2. 改錯 Leo and Kevin is my friends. → Leo and Kevin are my friends.
  3. 改錯 She not is tired today. → She isn't tired today. / She is not tired today.
  4. 改錯 Are Amy a new student? → Is Amy a new student?
  5. 重組 My English teacher is very kind.
  6. 重組 Kevin and Ben are not in the gym.
  7. 重組 Are you and Leo in Class 701?
  8. 造句 Who is your best friend? → My best friend is Leo.
  9. 造句 How are you today? → I am happy today.
  10. 單字造句 happy → Make a sentence with the word "happy".
  11. 單字造句 classmate → Make a sentence with the word "classmate".
  12. 二選一 Which is better, a big class or a small class?（a big class／a small class）
  13. 搶答 Which be verb goes with "they": am, is or are?
  14. 口說 Who is your best friend, and what is your friend like?

💬 對話替換

A: Hi, I'm Amy. Are you new here?
B: Yes, I am. I'm Leo.
A: Are you {hungry}?
B: No, I'm not. Are you?
替換字：hungry / tired / sleepy / happy / busy

寫作句型：My name is {Amy}. I am {twelve} years old. My best friend is {Leo}. {He} is {funny}.
Write about you and your best friend.

U2　This, That, These, Those + a / an

What is this, and what are those?

🧩 文法公式卡：This / that / these / those + a / an

  • this / that + one thing → This is a pencil. That is an apple.
  • these / those + two or more things → These are pencils. Those are boxes.
  • a + consonant sound · an + vowel sound → a ruler, an eraser, an umbrella
  • Plurals: -s · -es · -y → -ies · child → children → books, boxes, dictionaries, children

重組句：

  1. This is an eraser, and that is a ruler.
  2. Are these your books on the desk?
  3. Those are not my notebooks.
  4. That is an old umbrella.
  5. These boxes are full of oranges.
  6. Is this your new backpack, Leo?

🔤 單字

🧽 eraser e/ras/er　📏 ruler ru/ler　✏️ pencil pen/cil　📓 notebook note/book　🎒 backpack back/pack　📦 box b/ox　☂️ umbrella um/brel/la　🍊 orange or/ange　🧒 child ch/ild　⌚ watch w/atch　📖 dictionary dic/tion/ar/y

📖 短文：The Lost and Found Box

Ms. Lin has a big box in her classroom. It is the Lost and Found Box. Today, the box is full!

"What is this?" Ms. Lin asks. It is a blue pencil case. "That is my pencil case!" says Amy. "Thank you!"

"And what are these?" Ms. Lin asks. These are two red rulers and an eraser. "Those are Leo's rulers," says Kevin. "But the eraser isn't Leo's."

Next, Ms. Lin finds an umbrella, three notebooks and an orange. "Is this your umbrella, Kevin?" "Yes, it is!" Kevin says. "And these notebooks are mine too."

Nobody wants the orange. "Hmm, this orange is very old," Ms. Lin says. The children laugh. Now the box is almost empty. Ms. Lin puts the box next to the door.

  1. 選擇 What is the first thing in Ms. Lin's hand? → a blue pencil case （a red ruler／an orange）
  2. 選擇 Whose rulers are in the box? → Leo's （Kevin's／Amy's）
  3. 選擇 What is very old? → the orange （the umbrella／the box）
  4. 完整句 Whose umbrella is in the box? → The umbrella is Kevin's.
  5. 完整句 How many notebooks are in the box? → Three notebooks are in the box.
  6. 完整句 Is the eraser Leo's? → No, the eraser isn't Leo's.
  7. 口說 What is in your backpack today?
  8. 口說 What are those things on the wall of your classroom?

❓ 題卡（12 張）

  1. 📖 課文 Whose pencil case is in the box? → The blue pencil case is Amy's.
  2. 📖 課文 What are the two red things in the box? → The two red things are Leo's rulers.
  3. 📖 課文 Is the umbrella Kevin's? → Yes, the umbrella is Kevin's.
  4. 📖 課文 What does Ms. Lin say about the orange? → Ms. Lin says this orange is old.
  5. 🧩 文法 What is this on Ms. Lin's desk? → This is an eraser. 提示：This · an eraser
  6. 🧩 文法 What are those near the door? → Those are umbrellas. 提示：Those · umbrellas
  7. 🧩 文法 Are these your notebooks? → Yes, these are my notebooks. 提示：these · notebooks
  8. 🧩 文法 What is that in Leo's backpack? → That is an orange. 提示：That · an orange
  9. 🔤 填空 Leo has two red ___ for math class. → rulers
  10. 🔤 填空 Kevin's ___ is blue, and it keeps him dry. → umbrella
  11. 🔤 填空 Please write your name in your ___. → notebook
  12. 🔤 填空 I have an ___ for lunch today. → orange

⚔️ Battle 題

  1. 改錯 This is a eraser. → This is an eraser.
  2. 改錯 These is my books. → These are my books.
  3. 改錯 That are two boxs. → Those are two boxes.
  4. 改錯 I have three childs. → I have three children.
  5. 重組 These are my new pencils and erasers.
  6. 重組 Is that an orange in your backpack?
  7. 重組 Those boxes are very heavy today.
  8. 造句 What is in your backpack today? → My backpack has two notebooks and an eraser.
  9. 造句 Point at something far away. What is that? → That is a clock.
  10. 單字造句 umbrella → Make a sentence with the word "umbrella".
  11. 單字造句 box → Make a sentence with the word "box".
  12. 二選一 Which is better for school, a backpack or a shopping bag?（a backpack／a shopping bag）
  13. 搶答 What is the plural of "box"?
  14. 口說 Show us three things on your desk. What are these?

💬 對話替換

A: Excuse me. What is that?
B: That is the Lost and Found Box.
A: Is this your {eraser}?
B: Yes, it is. Thank you!
替換字：eraser / ruler / pencil / notebook / umbrella

寫作句型：This is my {backpack}. These are my {pencils}. That is {an umbrella}.
Write about things in your classroom with this, these and that.

U3　There Is / There Are + Places

What is in your room?

🧩 文法公式卡：There is / there are + prepositions of place

  • There is + one thing → There is a cat under the table.
  • There are + two or more things → There are two books on the desk.
  • There isn't / There aren't any · Is there ...? / Are there any ...? → Are there any eggs? Yes, there are.
  • Place words: in · on · under · next to · behind · in front of · between · near → The cat is behind the sofa.
  • How many ... are there? → How many students are there? There are twenty-five.

重組句：

  1. There is a cat under the table.
  2. There are two windows in my bedroom.
  3. Is there a lamp on your desk?
  4. There aren't any eggs in the fridge.
  5. The sofa is between the lamp and the bookcase.
  6. How many chairs are there in the kitchen?

🔤 單字

🛏️ bedroom bed/room　💡 lamp l/amp　🪑 chair ch/air　🪟 window win/dow　🛋️ sofa so/fa　🍳 kitchen kitch/en　🧊 fridge fr/idge　⬇️ under un/der　🔙 behind be/hind　↔️ between be/tween　🏞️ park p/ark

📖 短文：Kevin's Room

Kevin lives in an apartment in Zhubei. There are three bedrooms in the apartment. Kevin's room is small, but Kevin loves it.

There is a bed next to the window. There is a desk in front of the bed. On the desk, there is a lamp, and there are two books. Kevin's cat, Mimi, is under the desk.

There isn't a TV in Kevin's room. The TV is in the living room, between the sofa and the bookcase.

There is a big fridge in the kitchen. Are there any eggs in the fridge? Yes, there are six. Kevin wants an egg sandwich!

Near the apartment, there is a park. There are many trees in the park. Kevin and Leo are often there after school.

  1. 選擇 Where is the bed in Kevin's room? → next to the window （under the desk／in the kitchen）
  2. 選擇 How many eggs are there in the fridge? → six （two／three）
  3. 選擇 Where is the TV? → in the living room （in Kevin's room／in the kitchen）
  4. 完整句 What is on Kevin's desk? → There is a lamp and there are two books on Kevin's desk.
  5. 完整句 Are there many trees in the park? → Yes, there are many trees in the park.
  6. 完整句 Where is the TV in the living room? → The TV is between the sofa and the bookcase.
  7. 口說 What is there in your bedroom?
  8. 口說 What is there near your school?

❓ 題卡（12 張）

  1. 📖 課文 How many bedrooms are there in Kevin's apartment? → There are three bedrooms in Kevin's apartment.
  2. 📖 課文 Where is Kevin's cat? → Kevin's cat is under the desk.
  3. 📖 課文 Is there a TV in Kevin's room? → No, there isn't a TV in Kevin's room.
  4. 📖 課文 What is near Kevin's apartment? → There is a park near Kevin's apartment.
  5. 🧩 文法 What is on your desk? → There is a lamp on my desk. 提示：There is · on
  6. 🧩 文法 How many students are there in your class? → There are twenty-five students in my class. 提示：There are · students
  7. 🧩 文法 Is there a park near your school? → Yes, there is a park near my school. 提示：there is · near
  8. 🧩 文法 Are there any eggs in your fridge? → No, there aren't any eggs in my fridge. 提示：aren't · any
  9. 🔤 填空 Mimi the cat is ___ the desk. → under
  10. 🔤 填空 There is a bed next to the ___. → window
  11. 🔤 填空 The TV is ___ the sofa and the bookcase. → between
  12. 🔤 填空 The eggs are in the ___. → fridge

⚔️ Battle 題

  1. 改錯 There are a cat under the table. → There is a cat under the table.
  2. 改錯 There is two lamps on the desk. → There are two lamps on the desk.
  3. 改錯 There isn't some eggs in the fridge. → There aren't any eggs in the fridge.
  4. 改錯 Is there any books on the bookcase? → Are there any books on the bookcase?
  5. 重組 There is a small lamp on my desk.
  6. 重組 Are there any chairs in the kitchen?
  7. 重組 There are two parks near our school.
  8. 造句 What is in your bedroom? → There is a bed and a desk in my bedroom.
  9. 造句 Where is your backpack now? → My backpack is under my chair.
  10. 單字造句 between → Make a sentence with the word "between".
  11. 單字造句 behind → Make a sentence with the word "behind".
  12. 二選一 Which is better for homework, a desk in the bedroom or a table in the kitchen?（a desk in the bedroom／a table in the kitchen）
  13. 搶答 Fill in: "___ there any apples in the box?" Is or Are?
  14. 口說 What is there near your home?

💬 對話替換

A: Is there a park near your home?
B: Yes, there is. It is next to my school.
A: Is there a {bookstore} near your home too?
B: No, there isn't.
替換字：bookstore / library / hospital / supermarket / bus stop

寫作句型：There is {a bed} in my room. There are {two windows}. My {desk} is {next to} the {bed}.
Write about your bedroom.

U4　Present Simple: I / You / We / They

What do you do every day?

🧩 文法公式卡：Present simple: I / you / we / they

  • Habits and facts: I / you / we / they + verb → We play basketball after school.
  • Negative: don't + verb → I don't like math.
  • Question: Do + subject + verb ...? → Do you like English? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Time words: every day · on Mondays · after school · at night → I read at night.

重組句：

  1. I get up at six every day.
  2. We don't go to school on Sundays.
  3. Do you and Leo walk to school?
  4. My friends and I play basketball after school.
  5. They don't eat breakfast at home.
  6. We practice the piano every night.
  7. What do you do on weekends?

🔤 單字

🥞 breakfast break/fast　🍱 lunch l/unch　🍝 dinner din/ner　📝 homework home/work　🗓️ weekend week/end　🌅 morning morn/ing　🏀 basketball bas/ket/ball　🎹 piano pi/an/o　📚 library li/brar/y　⏰ early ear/ly　🚌 bus b/us　👫 together to/geth/er

📖 短文：Our School Day

Hi, I'm Amy. My friends and I go to Zhubei Junior High. We get up early on school days. I get up at six thirty.

I eat breakfast with my family. We don't eat a big breakfast. We usually have eggs and milk. Then I take the bus to school.

At school, we have seven classes. Leo and I like English, but we don't like math very much. At noon, we eat lunch in the classroom. After lunch, we sleep for a short time.

After school, Leo and Kevin play basketball. I don't play basketball. I go to the library and do my homework. At night, I practice the piano.

On weekends, we don't go to school. My friends and I see a movie or ride bikes together. Do you like weekends? We love weekends!

  1. 選擇 How many classes do the students have? → seven （six／eight）
  2. 選擇 What do the students do after lunch? → sleep for a short time （play basketball／go home）
  3. 選擇 What do Amy and her friends do on weekends? → see a movie or ride bikes （go to school／do math homework）
  4. 完整句 What do Amy and her family usually have for breakfast? → Amy and her family usually have eggs and milk.
  5. 完整句 Do Amy and Leo like math? → No, Amy and Leo don't like math very much.
  6. 完整句 Do Amy and her family eat a big breakfast? → No, Amy and her family don't eat a big breakfast.
  7. 口說 What do you and your friends do at lunchtime?
  8. 口說 What do you usually do on weekends?

❓ 題卡（12 張）

  1. 📖 課文 When do Amy and her friends get up? → Amy and her friends get up early on school days.
  2. 📖 課文 What do Amy and Leo like? → Amy and Leo like English.
  3. 📖 課文 Where do the students eat lunch? → The students eat lunch in the classroom.
  4. 📖 課文 What do Leo and Kevin do after school? → Leo and Kevin play basketball after school.
  5. 🧩 文法 What time do you get up on school days? → I get up at six thirty. 提示：get up · six thirty
  6. 🧩 文法 Do you eat breakfast at home? → Yes, I eat breakfast at home. 提示：eat · breakfast
  7. 🧩 文法 What do you and your friends do after school? → We play basketball after school. 提示：We play · after school
  8. 🧩 文法 Do your parents watch TV at night? → No, my parents don't watch TV at night. 提示：don't watch · at night
  9. 🔤 填空 I eat ___ at seven in the morning. → breakfast
  10. 🔤 填空 We do our ___ after school. → homework
  11. 🔤 填空 We don't go to school on the ___. → weekend
  12. 🔤 填空 Kevin and I play ___ in the park. → basketball

⚔️ Battle 題

  1. 改錯 I doesn't like math. → I don't like math.
  2. 改錯 They plays basketball after school. → They play basketball after school.
  3. 改錯 Do you likes English? → Do you like English?
  4. 改錯 We not go to school on Sundays. → We don't go to school on Sundays.
  5. 重組 We eat lunch in the classroom every day.
  6. 重組 Do you take the bus to school?
  7. 重組 My friends and I don't watch TV.
  8. 造句 What do you do on weekends? → I visit my grandparents on weekends.
  9. 造句 What do you eat for breakfast? → I eat bread and drink milk for breakfast.
  10. 單字造句 homework → Make a sentence with the word "homework".
  11. 單字造句 library → Make a sentence with the word "library".
  12. 二選一 Which do you like better after school, playing sports or reading books?（playing sports／reading books）
  13. 搶答 Say the negative: "I like math."
  14. 口說 What do you do after school every day?

💬 對話替換

A: What do you do after school?
B: I play basketball with Leo.
A: Do you {read books} after school too?
B: No, I don't. I do my homework.
替換字：read books / watch TV / play games / ride a bike / go to the library

寫作句型：I get up at {six thirty}. I {eat breakfast} with my family. After school, I {play basketball}. I don't {watch TV} at night.
Write about your school day.

U5　Present Simple: He / She / It

What does your family do every day?

🧩 文法公式卡：Present simple: he / she / it

  • he / she / it + verb-s → Leo plays. Mom works. The dog sleeps.
  • -es / -ies / has: go → goes · watch → watches · study → studies · have → has
  • Negative: doesn't + verb → Grandma doesn't have a job.
  • Question: Does + subject + verb ...? → Does Leo play basketball? Yes, he does.
  • What / Where / When does ...? → Where does Leo's mother work?

重組句：

  1. My sister studies English every evening.
  2. Leo's father works in a restaurant.
  3. Does Amy take the bus to school?
  4. Grandma doesn't drink coffee in the morning.
  5. What time does the store open?
  6. The dog sleeps under the sofa all day.
  7. Ms. Lin teaches English at our school.

🔤 單字

👨 father fa/ther　👩 mother moth/er　👵 grandma grand/ma　🩺 nurse n/urse　🧑‍🍳 cook c/ook　🏢 office of/fice　🏥 hospital hos/pi/tal　🌆 evening eve/ning　🐶 dog d/og　🚶 walk w/alk　📖 study stud/y　📰 newspaper news/pa/per

📖 短文：Leo's Busy Family

Leo lives with his family in Zhubei. Everyone in Leo's family is busy.

Leo's father is a cook. He works in a restaurant. He goes to work at ten and comes home late. He doesn't eat dinner with the family on weekdays.

Leo's mother is a nurse. She works at a hospital near their home. She gets up at five thirty every morning. She watches the news before work.

Grandma doesn't have a job now. She walks in the park every morning. In the evening, she reads the newspaper and cooks dinner.

Leo's sister, Mia, studies English every evening. Leo plays basketball after school. And what about Lucky, the family dog? Lucky sleeps all day! Does Lucky work? No, he doesn't!

  1. 選擇 What is Leo's mother's job? → a nurse （a cook／a teacher）
  2. 選擇 What does Leo's mother do before work? → watches the news （walks the dog／reads the newspaper）
  3. 選擇 Who sleeps all day? → Lucky （Grandma／Mia）
  4. 完整句 Does Leo's father eat dinner with the family on weekdays? → No, Leo's father doesn't eat dinner with the family on weekdays.
  5. 完整句 What does Grandma do in the evening? → Grandma reads the newspaper and cooks dinner.
  6. 完整句 What does Leo do after school? → Leo plays basketball after school.
  7. 口說 What does your mother or father do every morning?
  8. 口說 What does your best friend like to do?

❓ 題卡（12 張）

  1. 📖 課文 Where does Leo's father work? → Leo's father works in a restaurant.
  2. 📖 課文 What time does Leo's mother get up? → Leo's mother gets up at five thirty.
  3. 📖 課文 What does Grandma do every morning? → Grandma walks in the park every morning.
  4. 📖 課文 When does Mia study English? → Mia studies English every evening.
  5. 🧩 文法 What does your mother do in the morning? → My mother cooks breakfast in the morning. 提示：cooks · breakfast
  6. 🧩 文法 Does your best friend play basketball? → Yes, my best friend plays basketball. 提示：plays · basketball
  7. 🧩 文法 What time does your school start? → My school starts at seven thirty. 提示：starts · seven thirty
  8. 🧩 文法 Does your father watch TV at night? → No, my father doesn't watch TV at night. 提示：doesn't watch · at night
  9. 🔤 填空 Leo's mother works at a ___ near home. → hospital
  10. 🔤 填空 Grandma reads the ___ in the evening. → newspaper
  11. 🔤 填空 Lucky is the family ___. → dog
  12. 🔤 填空 Leo's father is a ___ in a restaurant. → cook

⚔️ Battle 題

  1. 改錯 Leo play basketball after school. → Leo plays basketball after school.
  2. 改錯 My sister studys English every day. → My sister studies English every day.
  3. 改錯 She don't like coffee. → She doesn't like coffee.
  4. 改錯 Does Amy likes cats? → Does Amy like cats?
  5. 改錯 He watchs TV at night. → He watches TV at night.
  6. 重組 My mother goes to work by bus.
  7. 重組 Does your brother play the piano?
  8. 重組 Kevin doesn't eat breakfast at home.
  9. 造句 What does your best friend do after school? → My best friend plays games after school.
  10. 造句 What does your mother do on weekends? → My mother goes shopping on weekends.
  11. 單字造句 hospital → Make a sentence with the word "hospital".
  12. 單字造句 walk → Make a sentence with the word "walk".

U6　Present continuous: be + V-ing

🧩 文法公式卡：Present continuous: am / is / are + V-ing

  • Negative isn't / aren't + V-ing → Kevin isn't sleeping.
  • Questions Are you + V-ing? · What is he doing? → What are you doing?

📖 短文：Amy's Video Call

  1. 選擇 Where is Leo this weekend? → in Tainan （in Zhubei／at school）

U7　Can / can't: ability and permission

🧩 文法公式卡：Can / can't: ability and permission

  • can + base verb → Amy can sing. (not: can sings)
  • can't = cannot → Leo can't sing at all.
  • Questions Can + subject + base verb? → Can you swim? Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
  • Permission Can I ...? · requests Can you ...? → Can I use your pen?

  1. Kevin can speak three languages.

  6. Can you help me with my homework?

📖 短文：The Talent Show

U8　Wh-questions: what / who / where / how

How do we ask for information?

🧩 文法公式卡：Wh-questions

  • Wh-word + be + subject? → Where is Mia from?
  • Wh-word + do / does + subject + base verb? → Where does your uncle work?
  • what thing · who person · where place · when / what time time · how way
  • how old · how many · how much → How much is the bubble tea?
  • whose + noun → Whose bike is this? It's Amy's.

重組句：

  2. How old is your cousin?

  4. What time does Leo get up?

📖 短文：Meet Our New Student

This week, our school newspaper has an interview with a new student. Amy talks to Mia, a new girl in Ms. Lin's class.

Amy: Where are you from, Mia? Mia: I'm from Kaohsiung. My family lives in Zhubei now. Amy: How old are you? Mia: I'm thirteen. My birthday is in May. Amy: Who is your best friend here? Mia: Leo is! He sits next to me.

Amy: What is your hobby? Mia: I love drawing. I draw cats every day. Amy: When do you get up? Mia: I get up at six thirty. I take the bus to school. Amy: How do you like Zhubei? Mia: I like it a lot. The people are very friendly.

After the interview, Mia sees a wallet on the floor. "Whose wallet is this?" she asks. Amy looks at it. "It's Kevin's! He can't find it." Mia gives the wallet to Kevin. "Thank you, Mia!" Kevin says. "Welcome to our school!"

  5. 完整句 How does Mia go to school? → Mia takes the bus to school.
  6. 完整句 Who sits next to Mia? → Leo sits next to Mia.
  7. 口說 What question do you want to ask Mia?
  8. 口說 How do you like your school?
