Grammar 1（國一）題庫審核：單選・克漏字・閱讀測驗

每題粗體是正解，括號內是三個錯誤選項；遊戲裡會自動打亂順序。學生畫面全英文，本頁中文只是給老師看的標籤。

U1　Be Verbs: am / is / are

📝 單選 15 題

  1. My name is Amy, and I ___ twelve years old.
    → am （is／are／be）
  2. Leo and Kevin ___ in Class 701 this year.
    → are （is／am／be）
  3. Ms. Lin ___ our English teacher, and she is very kind.
    → is （are／am／be）
  4. My brother ___ a student. He is a cook in a restaurant.
    → isn't （aren't／am not／don't）
  5. A: Are you a new student? B: Yes, ___.
    → I am （I'm／you are／I is）
  6. A: Is your mother a doctor? B: No, ___. She is a nurse.
    → she isn't （she is／he isn't／she aren't）
  7. ___ Kevin and Ben twins? Yes, they are.
    → Are （Is／Am／Do）
  8. I ___ hungry now, so I don't want a sandwich.
    → am not （isn't／aren't／not am）
  9. The students ___ noisy today. They are very quiet.
    → aren't （isn't／am not／don't）
  10. ___ your English class fun? Yes, it is.
    → Is （Are／Am／Do）
  11. A: Where ___ my books, Mom? B: They're on your desk.
    → are （is／am／be）
  12. Amy and I are classmates. ___ in Class 701.
    → We're （We／Our／They're）
  13. Mr. Wang is my uncle. ___ a bus driver in Zhubei.
    → He's （He／His／She's）
  14. Ms. Lin teaches English at our school. She is an English ___.
    → teacher （student／doctor／cook）
  15. I want a big sandwich because I am very ___.
    → hungry （tired／sleepy／happy）

  2. (2) are （is／am／be）

  4. (4) Is （Are／Am／Do）

📖 閱讀測驗：An Email from Amy

Dear Emma, Hi! My name is Amy Chen, and I am your new e-pal. I am twelve years old, and I am a student at Zhubei Junior High in Taiwan. I am in Class 701. My school is big, and my classmates are friendly.

My family is small. My mom is a doctor, and my dad is a cook. They are very busy, but they are always kind to me. My brother, Tom, is ten. He isn't a junior high student. He is in elementary school. He is noisy, but he is funny too.

My best friend is Leo. He is tall, and he is a great basketball player. We are in the same class. Our English teacher, Ms. Lin, is my favorite teacher. Her class is never boring.

Are you a student too? How old are you? Is your family big or small? Please write back soon! Love, Amy

  1. Why does Amy write this email?
    → to say hello to a new e-pal （to ask her teacher for help／to invite Leo to a game／to tell Emma about a trip）
  2. What is Amy's dad's job?
    → He is a cook. （He is a doctor.／He is a teacher.／He is a student.）
  3. Which is TRUE about Tom?
    → He is in elementary school. （He is twelve years old.／He is in Class 701.／He is quiet and shy.）
  4. The word "friendly" in paragraph 1 means…
    → kind and nice （big and tall／busy and tired／noisy and loud）

📖 閱讀測驗：A Visit to the Pet Shop

Today is Saturday. Mia and her brother Leo are at a pet shop in Zhubei. The shop is small, but it is full of animals.

"Look, Leo! What are those?" Mia asks. "Those are rabbits," says Mr. Hu, the shop owner. "They are only two months old." Near the door, Leo sees a box. "What is this?" he asks. "This is a mouse house," says Mr. Hu. "And these are the mice. They are very small."

Mia likes the fish. "Is that an orange fish?" she asks. "No, it isn't. It is a goldfish," Mr. Hu says. Over the door, there is a green bird. "That is a parrot. Its name is Kiki, and it is ten years old."

Mia and Leo want a pet, but their mom says, "A rabbit, yes. A mouse, no!" So today, Mia and Leo take home a white rabbit. They name it Snow.

  1. What is the story mainly about?
    → a visit to a pet shop （a day at the zoo／a new school bag／a rabbit in a park）
  2. How old are the rabbits?
    → two months old （ten years old／two years old／ten months old）
  3. What animals are in the box near the door?
    → mice （rabbits／goldfish／parrots）
  4. Why do Mia and Leo take home a rabbit, not a mouse?
    → Their mom doesn't want a mouse. （The mice are too old.／The rabbit is free.／Mr. Hu says no.）

Another robot can cook. It can make fried rice in ten minutes! But it can't taste the food, so it can't tell if the rice is too salty. A small dog robot can run and jump, but it can't climb stairs.

At the show, the students asked, "Can we touch the robots?" The guide said, "You can touch Bobo, but you can't touch the cooking robot. It is very hot." The students also asked, "Can robots feel happy or sad?" "No, they can't," the guide answered. "Robots can help people, but they can't feel like people."

After the show, Amy said, "Robots can do a lot, but they can't do everything!"

  1. What is the passage mainly about?
    → what robots can and can't do （how to cook fried rice／why robots are dangerous／a class trip to a zoo）
  2. What can Bobo do?
    → speak five languages （cook fried rice／climb stairs／feel happy or sad）
  3. Why can't the students touch the cooking robot?
    → It is very hot. （It is too small.／It can't speak.／It is sleeping.）
  4. In the passage, "salty" probably means …
    → having too much salt （being very cold／being very sweet／having no taste）

U8　Wh-questions: what / who / where / how

📝 單選 15 題

  1. A: ___ is your birthday? B: It's on May 5.
    → When （Where／Who／How）
  2. A: ___ does your uncle work? B: He works at a hospital.
    → Where （When／Who／How）
  3. A: ___ is your cousin? B: She is thirteen.
    → How old （How many／How much／How long）
  4. A: ___ bike is this? B: It's Amy's.
    → Whose （Who／Who's／Where）
  5. A: ___ is the bubble tea? B: It's sixty dollars.
    → How much （How many／How old／What time）
  6. A: ___ students are in your class? B: Twenty-eight.
    → How many （How much／How old／What）
  7. A: Where ___ Leo live? B: He lives in Zhubei.
    → does （do／is／are）
  8. What time ___ you get up on school days?
    → do （does／are／is）
  9. A: ___ is your English teacher? B: Ms. Lin is.
    → Who （Whose／What／Where）
  10. Where does your best friend ___?
    → live （lives／living／to live）
  11. A: How old ___ your sisters? B: They are ten and twelve.
    → are （is／do／does）
  12. A: ___ do you go to school? B: By bus.
    → How （What／Where／Who）
  13. A: What ___ your favorite food? B: Beef noodles.
    → is （are／do／does）
  14. A: Whose ___ is this? B: It's Kevin's. He keeps his money in it.
    → wallet （ruler／eraser／pencil）
  15. A: Where is Mia ___? B: She's from Kaohsiung.
    → from （to／at／on）

📄 克漏字：A New Friend at School

Hi, I'm Jenny. There is a new girl in my class this week. Her name is Rosa. At lunch, I ask her some questions. "(1) are you from?" "I'm from Tainan." "(2) old are you?" "I'm twelve, and my birthday is next week!" "(3) do you come to school?" "My mom drives me."

"Who (4) your best friend in Tainan?" "Her name is Lily. We talk on the phone every night." Then I see a pink pencil case on the floor. "(5) pencil case is this?" I ask. "Oh, it's mine. Thank you!" Rosa says. I think we can be good friends.

  1. (1) Where （What／Who／When）
  2. (2) How （What／Who／Where）
  3. (3) How （Who／Whose／What）
  4. (4) is （are／does／do）
  5. (5) Whose （Who／Who's／What）

📖 閱讀測驗：The School Lost and Found

Every week, many things go to the lost and found at Zhubei Junior High. This week, there are three umbrellas, two jackets, a blue water bottle and a phone. Whose things are these? The school wants to find their owners.

Where is the lost and found? It is in the school office on the first floor. When can you go there? You can go at lunchtime, from 12:00 to 12:40, or after school, from 4:00 to 4:30, Monday to Friday. Who can help you? Ms. Chen, the office worker, can.

How can you get your things back? First, tell Ms. Chen what you lost and where you lost it. Then, answer her questions, like "What color is your jacket?" or "How many stickers are on your water bottle?"

Please check the lost and found soon. After two weeks, the school gives the things to a charity.

  1. What is the purpose of the notice?
    → to help students find lost things （to sell old jackets to students／to invite students to lunch／to tell students about a teacher）
  2. Where is the lost and found?
    → in the school office （in the library／in the gym／in Ms. Lin's classroom）
  3. When can students NOT go to the lost and found?
    → on Saturday morning （at 12:10 on Monday／at 4:15 on Tuesday／at 12:30 on Friday）
  4. In the passage, "owners" means …
    → the people the things belong to （the workers in the school office／the teachers at the school／the people at a charity）

  4. In the passage, "extra" probably means …

📖 閱讀測驗：My Weekend in Taipei

Last weekend, my family and I visited Taipei. We left Zhubei early on Saturday morning and took the high-speed train. The trip only took thirty minutes!

First, we went to Taipei 101. We bought tickets and rode a fast elevator to the top. The view was amazing, and I took many photos of the city. Then, we walked to a small restaurant and ate beef noodles for lunch. My little brother didn't like the spicy soup, so he drank a lot of water.

In the afternoon, we visited the National Palace Museum. We saw many old and beautiful things there, and my dad bought a postcard for my grandma. Finally, we stayed at a hotel near the station.

On Sunday, it rained all day, so we didn't go to the zoo. We played cards in the hotel and came home in the evening. It was a short trip, but we had a great time!

  1. What is the blog post mainly about?
    → a family trip to Taipei （a visit to a big zoo／how to cook beef noodles／a train ride to Kaohsiung）
  2. How did the family go to Taipei?
    → by high-speed train （by car／by bus／by plane）
  3. Why didn't the family go to the zoo?
    → It rained all day. （The zoo was closed.／They were too tired.／They didn't have tickets.）
  4. In the passage, "view" means …
    → what you can see from a place （a kind of train ticket／a fast elevator／an old picture）
