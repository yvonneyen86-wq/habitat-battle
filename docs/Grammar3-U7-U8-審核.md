## U7 比較級最高級進階：as…as / The＋比較級（L16，12/15）

遊戲內單元名稱：Comparison: as ... as, The More ... The More

### 單選 15 題

| # | 題目 | 正解 | 誘答 | 考點 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kevin is as ＿＿＿ as his older brother now. | **tall** | taller ／ tallest ／ more tall | as + 原級 + as |
| 2 | This test is not as ＿＿＿ as the last one. | **difficult** | more difficult ／ most difficult ／ difficulter | not as + 原級 + as |
| 3 | I have as ＿＿＿ comic books as my cousin. | **many** | much ／ more ／ most | as many + 可數 N + as |
| 4 | My sister drinks as ＿＿＿ milk as I do every morning. | **much** | many ／ more ／ most | as much + 不可數 N + as |
| 5 | Amy runs as ＿＿＿ as Ben. They always finish the race together. | **fast** | faster ／ fastest ／ the fastest | as + 副詞 + as |
| 6 | My hair is as long as ＿＿＿. | **my sister's** | my sister ／ sister ／ my sister has | 對等比較（my sister's） |
| 7 | Ben is taller than Tom. = Tom is not as ＿＿＿ as Ben. | **tall** | taller ／ short ／ shorter | 互換：比較級 → not as…as（主詞對調） |
| 8 | This bag is not as expensive as that one. = That bag is ＿＿＿ this one. | **more expensive than** | as expensive as ／ not as expensive as ／ the most expensive of | 互換：not as…as → 比較級 |
| 9 | Mount Jade is higher than any other ＿＿＿ in Taiwan. | **mountain** | mountains ／ the mountains ／ a mountains | than any other + 單數 N |
| 10 | Ivy is the tallest girl in our class. = Ivy is taller than ＿＿＿ girl in our class. | **any other** | any others ／ all other ／ other | 互換：最高級 → than any other |
| 11 | ＿＿＿ you practice, the better you will play. | **The more** | More ／ The most ／ Much | The + 比較級（前半句） |
| 12 | The harder you study, ＿＿＿ your grades will be. | **the higher** | higher ／ the highest ／ high | the + 比較級（後半句） |
| 13 | The earlier you get up, the ＿＿＿ time you have. | **more** | much ／ most ／ many | The + 比較級（more time） |
| 14 | The more you sleep, ＿＿＿. | **the better you feel** | you feel better ／ better you feel ／ the best you feel | 兩半句都要 the + 比較級 |
| 15 | A: When do you need the report? B: The sooner, ＿＿＿. | **the better** | the best ／ better ／ good | The sooner, the better |

### 克漏字：Twins in Class 903

Ella and Emma are twins in Class 903. Ella is as [1] as Emma; they are both 158 cm. Emma has 200 books, and Ella has 200 books, too. Ella has as [2] books as Emma.

But the twins are different in some ways. Ella is not as [3] as Emma at math, so Emma often helps her. Their mom always says, "The more you help each other, the [4] you will both learn." Emma is better at math than any other [5] in their class, and Ella is the best singer in the school.

| 格 | 正解 | 誘答 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1] | **tall** | taller ／ tallest ／ more tall |
| [2] | **many** | much ／ more ／ most |
| [3] | **good** | better ／ best ／ well |
| [4] | **more** | most ／ much ／ many |
| [5] | **student** | students ／ the students ／ a students |

### 閱讀測驗：Which Phone Should Leo Buy?

Leo wants a new phone, so he compares two of them. Phone A costs NT$9,000, and Phone B costs NT$9,000, too. Phone A is as cheap as Phone B, but the two phones are different in other ways.

Phone B is not as heavy as Phone A, so it is easier to carry. But Phone A's camera is better than Phone B's, and its battery lasts longer. Leo loves taking photos, and he often forgets to charge his phone.

His sister says, "The more photos you take, the more you need a good camera." Leo agrees with her, and he buys Phone A.

| # | 題目 | 正解 | 誘答 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is the passage mainly about? | **how Leo compares two phones and chooses one** | how to take good photos ／ why phones are so expensive ／ why Leo's sister buys a phone |
| 2 | Which sentence is TRUE? | **Phone A is as cheap as Phone B.** | Phone A is cheaper than Phone B. ／ Phone B is heavier than Phone A. ／ Phone B's camera is better than Phone A's. |
| 3 | Why does Leo choose Phone A? | **Its camera and battery are better.** | It is lighter than Phone B. ／ It is cheaper than Phone B. ／ His sister has the same phone. |
| 4 | What does Leo's sister mean? | **People who take many photos need a good camera.** | A good camera can take more photos. ／ Leo should take fewer photos. ／ Cameras are more important than phones. |

## U8 情態助動詞進階：could / would / might / had better（L17，12/22）

遊戲內單元名稱：Modals: could, would, might, had better

### 單選 15 題

| # | 題目 | 正解 | 誘答 | 考點 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When I was six, I ＿＿＿ ride a bike. | **could** | can ／ might ／ had better | could = 過去能力 |
| 2 | ＿＿＿ you help me carry these books, please? | **Could** | Had ／ Might ／ Did | Could you…? 禮貌請求 |
| 3 | It's very cloudy. It ＿＿＿ rain this afternoon. | **might** | had better ／ would like ／ mights | might = 可能性 |
| 4 | You have a fever. You ＿＿＿ stay home and rest. | **had better** | had better to ／ would like ／ had not better | had better + 原形 |
| 5 | The road is icy. You ＿＿＿ drive fast. | **had better not** | had not better ／ had better not to ／ would like | had better not + 原形 |
| 6 | I ＿＿＿ a glass of water, please. | **would like** | would like to ／ would ／ had better | would like + N |
| 7 | A: Would you like some cookies? B: ＿＿＿ | **Yes, please.** | Yes, I would like. ／ Yes, I do. ／ Yes, I like. | Would you like…? → Yes, please. |
| 8 | A: Would you like to go hiking with us? B: ＿＿＿ | **Yes, I'd love to.** | Yes, I would like. ／ Yes, I like. ／ No, I don't. | Would you like to…? → Yes, I'd love to. |
| 9 | He ＿＿＿ swim when he was young, but now he can. | **couldn't** | can't ／ mightn't ／ hadn't better | couldn't = 過去不會 |
| 10 | Kate's light is still on. She ＿＿＿ be busy now. | **might** | mights ／ might to ／ is might | might + 原形（不加 s） |
| 11 | I would like ＿＿＿ the school band next year. | **to join** | join ／ joining ／ joined | would like + to V |
| 12 | Maybe Tom is at home. = Tom ＿＿＿ at home. | **might be** | might is ／ might ／ is might | 改寫：Maybe … → might be |
| 13 | You should see a dentist. = You ＿＿＿ see a dentist. | **had better** | had better to ／ has better ／ had better not | 改寫：should → had better |
| 14 | Which is the most polite way to ask? ＿＿＿ | **Could you open the door, please?** | Can you open the door? ／ Open the door. ／ You open the door. | 禮貌程度階梯 |
| 15 | Would you mind ＿＿＿ the window? It's cold. | **closing** | to close ／ close ／ closed | Would you mind + V-ing（回扣 L7） |

### 克漏字：A Rainy Saturday

On Saturday morning, Mia wanted to go to the beach with her friends. Her dad looked at the dark, cloudy sky and said it [1] rain later. Then he told Mia she [2] take an umbrella.

At the beach, it started to rain hard, and Mia [3] find her umbrella in her bag. She ran into a small shop. The owner asked, "[4] you like a cup of hot tea?" Mia drank the tea and smiled. She said she would like [5] the beach again on a sunny day.

| 格 | 正解 | 誘答 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1] | **might** | had better ／ would like ／ mights |
| [2] | **had better** | had better to ／ would like ／ had not better |
| [3] | **couldn't** | can't ／ mightn't ／ hadn't better |
| [4] | **Would** | Could ／ Might ／ Had |
| [5] | **to visit** | visit ／ visiting ／ visited |

### 閱讀測驗：Ask Dr. Wu

Dear Dr. Wu, I can't sleep at night. I go to bed at eleven, but I am still awake at two. In the morning, I am so tired that I fall asleep in class. Could you give me some advice? Kevin

Dear Kevin, You might drink too much tea or cola in the afternoon, so you had better not drink them after 3 p.m. You had better not play games on your phone in bed, either. The light from the screen could keep you awake.

You had better go to bed at the same time every night, and you should get some exercise during the day. When I was your age, I couldn't sleep well either, but these things helped me a lot. Dr. Wu

| # | 題目 | 正解 | 誘答 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is Kevin's problem? | **He can't sleep at night.** | He drinks too much water. ／ He doesn't like his class. ／ He doesn't have a phone. |
| 2 | What might keep Kevin awake at night? | **tea, cola and his phone screen** | exercise during the day ／ going to bed at eleven ／ sleeping in class |
| 3 | Which is advice from Dr. Wu? | **Go to bed at the same time every night.** | Play games on your phone before bed. ／ Drink cola after 3 p.m. ／ Sleep in class in the morning. |
| 4 | What do we learn about Dr. Wu? | **He had the same problem when he was young.** | He can't sleep well now. ／ He is Kevin's father. ／ He never gets any exercise. |

## 請老師特別看的幾處

出題範圍沒有超出兩頁教案與練習卷，但以下是我做的判斷，請確認。

1. **U7 第 7、8、10 題（句型互換）**：用「=」連接兩句改成單選，因為遊戲每題只能有一個空格，無法做成改寫題。
2. **U7 第 7 題**：誘答放了 short／shorter。文法上 not as short as 也通，只是意思相反（變成 Ben 比較矮），要靠語意判斷。若您希望只考形式，我把它換成 more tall 之類的錯誤形式。
3. **U7 閱讀測驗第 2 題**：正解「Phone A is as cheap as Phone B.」直接引用課文原句；若希望改成同義改寫（例：The two phones cost the same.）以避免學生找字對答，請告訴我。
4. **U8 第 15 題（Would you mind + V-ing）**：教案只在 Step 2 一行回扣 L7，我出了 1 題。不想在這單元考的話我換成 had better 或禮貌請求。
5. **U8 第 7、8 題（回答 Would you like…?）**：誘答「Yes, I do.」「No, I don't.」照練習卷的錯誤選項設計；文法本身沒錯，只是不是 Would you like 的正確回答。
6. **may 沒有單獨出題**：教案 Step 3 的確定程度表有 may，但目標與練習卷都沒有考 may，所以我沒出；需要的話我加 1 題。
7. **題目沒有照抄練習卷**：例句多半換了人名與情境；有幾題（如 My hair is as long as my sister's、Mount Jade、the sooner the better）與講義例句相同。若您希望遊戲題目與練習卷完全不重複或完全一致，請告訴我。
